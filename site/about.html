A family firm from Yenston, near Templecombe, building walls, steps and gardens in stone since 1991.

Our story

Yenstone has been building in stone since 1991, and we are still doing it the same way: by hand, one stone at a time.

We are a family firm, working from Perhams Farm at Yenston, near Templecombe. Dry stone walling is where we started and it is still the heart of what we do. It is slow, skilled work that rewards doing it properly.

Everything else we build, from steps and terraces to paving and whole gardens, is shaped by that way of working. We plan the walls, the surfaces and the planting together, in local stone, so the finished garden feels settled from the first season.

Questions we are often asked

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Call us on 01963 371123 or send the quote form. We arrange a visit to look at the site, talk through what you want and send you a written quote.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across Somerset and Dorset from our base at Yenston, near Templecombe, including Wincanton, Sherborne, Yeovil, Gillingham, Shaftesbury and Sturminster Newton.

What is dry stone walling?

A dry stone wall is built without mortar. Each stone is selected and fitted by hand so the weight of the wall holds it together. Done properly, it can last for generations.

Can you repair an existing stone wall?

Yes. We repair and rebuild damaged walls, including boundary walls, retaining walls and lime-mortared walls, reusing the original stone wherever we can.

Do you carry out insurance work?

Yes, we do insurance work on walls. Tell us what happened and we will explain what is involved and give you a quote you can pass to your insurer.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the size and the ground we are working with. A short repair can be a matter of days, a whole garden takes longer. We give you a timescale in the quote.
