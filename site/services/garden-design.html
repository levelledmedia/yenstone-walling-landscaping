Plan the whole garden, then build it in order

Some gardens need a tidy-up. Others need a fresh start: new levels, a proper terrace, steps that make sense, borders that frame the view instead of hiding it. We look at how you use the space and how the land falls before we draw anything.

We plan the walls, paving and planting together, so it feels like one garden rather than a set of separate jobs. Then we agree the order of work, so each stage leads naturally into the next.

What a plan covers

A plan can be as simple as a sketch for one border or as full as a whole-garden scheme. It always starts with a visit.

Levels and layout

How the garden falls, where it needs to be level, and how you move through it.

Walls and boundaries

Stone walls that hold the ground, mark the edges and give the planting something to grow against.

Paving and surfaces

Terraces, paths and drives chosen to suit the house and laid to match the plan.

Planting and borders

Beds and borders that frame the view, planned alongside the hard landscaping so they sit together.

Not sure where to start? Call us. We will come and look at the garden, talk through what you want and explain how we would approach it.
